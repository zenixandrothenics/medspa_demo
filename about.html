Our story

Quietly obsessive about skin.

A nurse-led studio, a physician-backed standard and a belief that the best aesthetics are the ones you can't quite name.

Est. 2014 · Old Town Scottsdale 12 years · 8,400+ treatments
Founder

“My job is to make you look like you slept.”

A note from Elena

A small studio with a strong point of view.

I opened Lumière in 2014 with a treatment room, a very good lamp and a growing frustration: too many people were being sold a face they never asked for.

Twelve years later, the studio is bigger, the technology is better, and the philosophy hasn't moved. We assess before we inject. We say no when no is the right answer. We plan in months and years, not in packages and promotions.

Our medical director, Dr. Rachel Colletti, reviews every protocol and is available for any concern. Our aesthetician, Maya, handles the skin side of the practice with a patience that borders on the meditative. And I still do every injectable consultation myself.

If you're looking for the biggest lips on Instagram, we're probably not your studio. If you're looking for skin that looks like yours, only better rested — come in. We'll talk.

E. Elena Marchetti, MSN, APRN Founder & Lead Aesthetic Injector

What we stand for

Three rules we don't bend.

i.

Restraint

The right dose is the smallest one that works. We would rather see you twice than overcorrect once.

ii.

Science first

Board-certified oversight, medical-grade products and evidence over trends. If the data isn't there, we wait.

iii.

The long view

Skin is a decades-long project. We build plans that age well, not plans that photograph well for a week.

Milestones

A decade, slowly built.

2014

One room, one nurse

Elena opens a single treatment room on Marshall Way with a focus on conservative neuromodulator work.

2017

Medical oversight, formalised

Dr. Rachel Colletti joins as medical director, bringing hospital-grade protocols to the practice.

2021

Skin, in-house

Maya Chen joins to build the skin programme — microneedling, peels and a very loyal following.

2024

A new home on Stetson

The studio moves to its current home: four rooms, a private entrance and considerably better coffee.

2026

Still saying no

8,400 treatments later, the most common thing we hear is still: “thank you for talking me out of it.”
