The Lumière philosophy

A quieter kind of beauty.

Subtle by design.
Never by accident.

We believe the best work is the work no one can quite put their finger on. Skin that looks rested. Features that look like yours — only more awake.

Founded by an aesthetic nurse and guided by a board-certified medical director, Lumière pairs clinical precision with an editorial eye. Every treatment begins with a conversation, a plan and a promise: subtle, considered, never overdone.

Our Old Town studio is calm by design — unhurried appointments, honest recommendations and a team that would rather talk you out of a treatment than sell you one you don't need.
